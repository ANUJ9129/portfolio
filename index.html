About me

From real problems to measurable outcomes.

I'm a Product Manager who has built products across healthcare, fintech, B2B SaaS, AI, and mobility.

I started in consulting, working on large-scale transformation problems across banking, healthcare, and EV mobility. That experience taught me how to break down complex workflows, use data to find the real problem, and work across business and technical teams to drive change.

I then moved closer to the product itself. At Lynkr, I worked in an early-stage B2B SaaS environment, speaking directly with customers, identifying pain points, defining requirements, and partnering with engineers to build and launch 0→1 products and workflow solutions.

Today at CVS Health, I build and improve digital healthcare products, including AI-enabled workflows, drug search and pricing experiences, pharmacy tools, enrollment journeys, APIs, automation, and product analytics.

Across each step, I've moved closer to what I enjoy most: understanding users, building products from real problems, working closely with engineering, and turning ideas into measurable outcomes.
