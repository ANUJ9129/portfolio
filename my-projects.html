What it is
A copilot for support agents. It reads a customer message, looks up the account and company policies, drafts a grounded reply, and proposes actions like refunds that only happen after a person approves.
How I built it
Ten phases, each with measured results: evals, RAG, tools with human approval, a billing agent that pauses for sign-off, churn prediction, analytics, and security testing. I wrote down 74 product decisions and the reason for each.
